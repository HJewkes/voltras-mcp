# Understand your data

These pages explain what the numbers from a session mean. They are background reading, not steps
to follow: the [guides](/guides/) cover how to do things, and the [reference](/reference/) lists
every tool.

- [Velocity and effort](/concepts/velocity-and-effort): what bar speed and velocity loss say about
  reps in reserve and RPE, and why the server will not state an effort number without a curve
  fitted to you.
- [Calibration and trust](/concepts/calibration-and-trust): which numbers are measured and which
  are derived, why trends beat single readings, and where the defaults are not yours.

Four more pages are planned: fatigue and pacing, technique signals, how coaching works, and privacy
and local data.
