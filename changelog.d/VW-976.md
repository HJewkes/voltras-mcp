- `profile.set_training_background` takes `returnClearance` (`cleared`, `cleared_with_limit` or `not_yet`) and `returnClearanceOn`, the answer to the return clearance question after a break. Only the answer and its date are stored, never what a limit is, and the later-dated answer stands. `plan.next_workout` carries it as `reEntry.clearance` during a break or its window, with a `blocked` reason on `not_yet`, and `session.start` refuses a working session while that holds (VW-976).
