- Developer tooling: the fidelity build's overlay outlines every tagged component, coloured by where it comes from, and merges wrappers that share a box into one stacked label. Its legend counts each kind, lists dashboard-local components as titan promotion candidates beside the ones the build could not tag, and copies the lot as JSON. It is off by default and toggles from the pill, `?fidelity=1` or Alt+Shift+F (VW-798).
