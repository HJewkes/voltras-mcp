---
section: Changed
---

- The planning brief's deload-cadence advisory now splits by tier. A beginner gets none. An
  intermediate is read against 4:1 or 5:1, and a lifter with no deload on record is told to
  commit to 3 weeks and extend week by week. A declared advanced lifter is read against 3:1 or
  4:1, with the week-4 exception named, and 6 or more weeks without a deload adds a
  `tier_evidence` note that never changes the tier. A new `active_rest` advisory names a missing
  deload-plus-off-week in the ended weeks of the last 12 months once. `plan.template.create`
  returns a `week_without_off_day` warning, once per week, on the template that leaves the week
  with no day off; a rest template is not a training day. Every cadence line says the ratio is a planning prior and the trigger stays
  performance-based (VW-619).
