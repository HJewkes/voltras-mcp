section: Security
---

- `device.send_raw` and `debug.recent_frames` are no longer offered to Claude by default. They are registered only when the server runs with `VMCP_DEBUG_TOOLS=on`, so an ordinary session cannot send an unchecked command that moves the motor or pull raw telemetry into its transcript. `server.health` reports the setting as `debugTools` (VW-935).
