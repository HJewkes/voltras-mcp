---
section: Changed
---

- A changelog entry is now its own file, `changelog.d/<ticket>.md`, with its group in front
  matter, instead of an edit to `CHANGELOG.md`, so two pull requests with entries no longer
  conflict. `npm run changelog:check` validates the fragments and `npm run changelog:fold`
  folds them into a version section at release. Until then they do not show on this page
  (VW-704, #591).
