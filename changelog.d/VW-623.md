- Planning a week now checks how many days each muscle is trained against its recovery band: legs
  about twice a week; back, chest and triceps two or three times; biceps, forearms, side delts and
  calves three to five times. A beginner trains every muscle two to four times a week. The bands
  are planning priors, so a muscle outside one gets a suggestion and the plan is still saved. The
  muscle plan on the dashboard reports each muscle's planned and trained days this week, and the
  planning brief notes when the next block adds a training day before the current day count has
  held for two blocks (VW-623).
