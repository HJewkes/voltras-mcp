- Under `VMCP_CUES_MIDSET=risk`, the cue-delivery ledger records each set's risk reading (band,
  points, factor levels and veto ids) with every line decision. A refused mid-set line names why:
  `risk_band_amber`, `risk_band_red`, or `risk_reading_missing` when the set has no reading.
  `system.set_cues` under `risk` adds `liveSetRisk`, the band and factors of each active set.
  Under `off` and `on` the ledger and the `system.set_cues` reply are unchanged (VW-615).
