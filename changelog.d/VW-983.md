- When your dated block ended during a break, or every planned workout is done, `plan.next_workout` now offers the baseline session too, on the same days as for a planned workout: during the break and on the first day back. It takes its lifts from everything you trained on your last training day before then, and loads them at the lighter re-entry load. When none of those lifts has a load on record, it points at the starting prescription instead (VW-983).
