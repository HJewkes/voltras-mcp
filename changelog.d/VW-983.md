- When your dated block ended during a break, or every planned workout is done, `plan.next_workout` now offers the baseline session too. It takes its lifts from the last session you trained before the break, loads them at the lighter re-entry load, and keeps offering it while the break or the return window is open. When that session holds no lift with a load on record, it points at the starting prescription instead (VW-983).
