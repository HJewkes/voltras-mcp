- The coach can now pick one thing to work on from a finished set: full range when your
  range shrinks across the set, control on the way down when most reps drop fast with no
  pause at the bottom, and a smooth drive when most reps stall mid-lift. When several read
  at once it picks them in that order; a clean set or a one-rep set picks nothing. Nothing
  speaks it yet; a later change wires it into the cues (VW-605).
