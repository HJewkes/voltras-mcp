- After a break of 15 days to six months, `plan.next_workout` offers a short baseline session for the first day back: two or three lifts from the planned workout that you have trained before, one working set each at the lighter re-entry load, in an order where each set is lighter than the one before and the session ends on a lighter back-off set. It fits the 20-minute reduced-scope session. When no planned lift has a load on record, or after a longer break, it points at the starting prescription instead (VW-982).
