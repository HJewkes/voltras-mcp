- `plan.respond_deload_advisory` files the lifter's yes or no to a recovery rung that
  `plan.next_workout` advised. It records the answer and changes nothing else: accepting never
  edits the plan. A declined rung is not offered again on the same evidence; a newer session
  that shows the decline again brings it back. `deloadAdvisory` names the tool on `advise` only.
