---
section: Changed
---

- `plan.suggest_progression` now says how old "last time" is for that lift. Its new `lastTime` field gives the date and the days since, and marks it stale after 15 days. After a break of 15 days to six months, the suggestion no longer adds weight to the old load: it steps down to the re-entry load (80% of the last top working load after 15-28 days, a third of it after 29-182 days, rounded down to 5 lb and never under 5 lb) and names the research it comes from. After a longer break it holds and points at the starting prescription, and a stale "last time" never suggests adding a set (VW-907).
