- `session.checkin` takes a `feel` answer, a whole number from -5 to +5, given within 2 minutes of the session's last set; a later one comes back in `withheld`. On a climb-back ramp week, `plan.suggest_progression` holds the load and returns `returnRamp.setDelta: -1` after two return sessions in a row rated below 0, and holds the load when the latest `soreness` answer was `medium` or `high`. A passing session now climbs at most one ramp step, so a held week is not made up by a jump (VW-993).
