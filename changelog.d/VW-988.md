section: Changed
---

- `plan.suggest_progression` now climbs a lift back after a short or medium break. Inside the return window a fresh basis holds at the window load (`reEntry`). After the window, `returnRamp` gives the week's load: equal weekly steps from the window load back to the lift's last working load before the break, over half the weeks off (2 to 12 weeks), never above that load (VW-988).
