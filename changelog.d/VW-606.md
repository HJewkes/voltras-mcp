- A new cue-delivery layer can now decide, per set, which coaching lines to say and how
  many: an intro and the current focus before the set, at most two lines during it (none
  for an advanced lifter, one for an intermediate, two for a beginner, and none at all
  with mid-set cues off), and a short debrief after it. Slowdown alerts and target calls
  share that allowance, a slowdown wins over a focus reminder, and reminders switch to a
  short form once the set reads near failure. It is not switched on yet; a later change
  wires it in behind a setting (VW-606).
