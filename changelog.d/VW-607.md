- `VMCP_CUE_DELIVERY=on` speaks the automatic cues through the new cue-delivery layer: at most
  two lines per pre-set, mid-set and post-set window, fewer mid-set lines for more experienced
  lifters, one technique focus repeated in the same words across sets, and a firmer tone as the
  set nears failure. It replaces the default cues rather than adding to them, and `VMCP_CUES`
  and `VMCP_CUES_MIDSET` still gate it. Off by default (VW-607).
