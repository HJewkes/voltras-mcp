- `plan.exercise.create` warns past the beginner tier when a session trains a specialized muscle with only one exercise; a specialized muscle gets two per session, a back-burner muscle one (VW-624).
- `plan.block.planning_brief` offers one more weekly session for a specialized muscle limited by fatigue (biceps, delts) when the block being planned is the last before an active rest. It never offers one for hamstrings, for a beginner or in a fat-loss phase, and it names the 2-mesocycle hold when the specialization is younger than that (VW-624).
