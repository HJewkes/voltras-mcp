- The dashboard serves the command trail at `GET /api/activity`: every write an agent or a
  wall tap made, newest first, with who acted, the tool, a short summary in fitness units,
  the outcome and the session. You can filter by session, flow, actor, outcome and time,
  and page through it 200 rows at most at a time. A wall tap's row now records its summary
  and the open session too. See [Dashboard API](/reference/dashboard-api#command-trail-api-activity) (VW-893).
