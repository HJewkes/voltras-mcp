section: Changed
---

- `rep_finalized` and `set_ended` push events are compact by default. A rep push carries its attributes, summary line and peak force; a set close carries one table row per rep (`rep_table`) instead of the per-rep objects, and its `set` block drops the ids already on the attributes, the deprecated `training_mode` alias and the always-null `active_mode`. `VMCP_PUSH_DETAIL=full` restores the full payloads. Spoken cues and the dashboard still read full detail, and `set.get` returns it on demand ([push events](/reference/push-events)) (VW-832).
